About Me

Hello! I'm Evan - a software engineer, musician, and golfer based in Denver.

I was born and raised in Grand Rapids, but I currently live in Denver, Colorado.
I'm currently working for Crowe as a Software Engineer.

I graduated from Michigan State University in 2022 with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering and a minor in Entrepreneurship and Innovation.

During my time at MSU, I was a proud member of the Spartan Marching Band's trumpet section, placing as high as fifth chair out of 60.
I've played trumpet for over 10 years, performed for over a hundred thousand people across seven states and three countries!

These days, I keep that passion going by learning guitar, collecting vinyl, and diving into anything with great tone and rhythm - Cory Wong is a longtime favorite musician of mine.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me on a golf course, hiking, or cycling around town.

I also spend plenty of time with my dog, Jedha, who never gets tired of playing fetch and keeps life fun.

I'm always looking for new challenges and opportunities to grow as a developer, musician, and person. Feel free to connect with me!
